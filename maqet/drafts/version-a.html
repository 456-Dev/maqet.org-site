how it works

One prompt, 24 hours, 60 seconds of film. Here's the weekend, start to finish.

  1. Sat Nov 7 · 11:00 AM

    The prompt drops

    Shared with everyone, in person. Location to be announced. The 24-hour clock starts.

  2. Sat → Sun · 24 hrs

    Write. Shoot. Edit.

    Any camera, any genre. Script it, film it and cut it before the clock runs out. Sleep optional.

  3. Sun Nov 8 · 11:00 AM

    Final cut due

    Submit your finished 60-second film. The clock stops.

  4. INT. Star Bar · 7:00 PM

    Screening night

    Sunday, Nov 8. Every film screens together, and our expert panel picks the winners.

the rules

any camera any genre anything 60 seconds
  • 60 seconds. Your film runs one minute.
  • Use the prompt. It's revealed in person at 11:00 AM on Saturday, Nov 7.
  • Make it in the window. All writing, shooting and editing happens between 11:00 AM Saturday and 11:00 AM Sunday.
  • Teams of 3 or more. No team yet? Register solo and we'll help connect you with one.
  • Submit by 11:00 AM Sunday. Final submissions close Sunday, Nov 8 at 11:00 AM.
  • Everything screens. All works play together at Star Bar on Sunday at 7PM.

questions

Do I need a team to sign up?
No. Register solo and we'll help connect you with a team. Teams need at least 3 people.
What can I shoot on?
Any camera. Phone, DSLR, camcorder or cinema camera: if it records video, it counts.
Where do we get the prompt?
In person at 11:00 AM on Saturday, Nov 7. Location to be announced.
Can we start before the prompt?
Get your team and gear ready ahead of time. The writing, shooting and editing all happen after the prompt drops.
Who picks the winners?
An expert panel. First place takes $250, second $100 and third $50.
When and where is the screening?
Sunday, Nov 8 at 7PM at Star Bar. Every finished film screens together.
