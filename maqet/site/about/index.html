ABOUT US

MAQET is a community-based artist collective in NYC that creates third spaces for artists. Our collective is founded on the belief that a strong and supportive artistic community uplifts all its members. We believe in the power of group advocacy to create the change that we want to see in the art world and beyond.

MAQUETTE is a model or preliminary sketch of a bigger dream. Our collective is the foundation from which artists present their works and develop an audience. We believe that creative expression is valid and valuable in and of itself, and respect artwork that shows creative vision, intention, and ambition. Our goal is to empower artists through the active cultivation of community, placing control of artistic work back in the hands of creators. Artists do not have to pay or prove their commercial worth to enter our spaces.

MAKE IT. We create together. Our group began as a student-driven initiative to give graduating seniors at Parsons an opportunity to present their thesis work in a gallery space. Now we continue that mission by building space for artists who seek to push boundaries, and step beyond convention.
