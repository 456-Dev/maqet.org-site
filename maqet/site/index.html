PAST SHOWS

TIME BEFORE/TIME AFTER

2026

We exist always on a precarious fulcrum between the time that came before and the time that will come after. We struggle to float in a constant, everlasting sea of change while fixed in the isolated point of the present. You are invited to experience each artwork in its singular, precipitous moment. You alone hold this second for yourself; together we hang suspended between time before and time after

DOORJAMBS: HOME & BORDERS

2026

DOORJAMBS: Home & Borders explores home as an unstable, evolving concept shaped by citizenship, belonging, displacement, and borders. The exhibition considers home not as a fixed place, but as something continually constructed, questioned, lost, and reclaimed.
